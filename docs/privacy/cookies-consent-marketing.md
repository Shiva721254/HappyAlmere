# Cookies, consent and marketing

> HA-010-A documentation contract, 21 November 2026. [Authority](https://github.com/Shiva721254/HappyAlmere/issues/33#issuecomment-7065334379); [phase evidence and dispositions](../phases/HA-010-A.md). Documentation is not production certification, an operational exercise, or permission to process customer data.

PROD-OPEN-09–12; PROD-A04. Accepted source has local CSS/Razor, no script tags in shared layout, CSP script-src none, no consent/analytics/advertising integration and no public authentication cookie service. Antiforgery is configured Secure/HttpOnly/Strict. This source inventory does not prove every runtime cookie, browser storage, proxy or provider log; fresh browser evidence and a live inventory are separate gates.

Before activation inventory name/purpose/provider/expiry, domain/path, cookie/storage/SDK/network endpoints, recipients and basis. Essential session/antiforgery or purpose-limited settings require purpose-specific exemption/GDPR assessment; no banner is needed solely for appearances. See [AP cookies](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en/themes/internet-and-smart-devices/cookies) and [banner guidance](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/themas/internet-slimme-apparaten/cookies/heldere-en-misleidende-cookiebanners), refreshed2026-11-21.

Tracking, fingerprinting, behavioral advertising and third-party SDKs remain off initially. Any future activation needs separate owner/legal/privacy authority, prior informed granular consent where required, no prechecked optional categories, refusal as easy as acceptance, versioned minimal proof and easy withdrawal that stops future processing. Scrolling/inactivity is not consent. No raw searches or persistent precise location by default, including diagnostic/proxy leakage; manual location alternative required.

Electronic marketing has separate Dutch rules: prior opt-in or specifically justified existing-customer exception, clear sender/purpose and functioning unsubscribe/suppression; service notices cannot disguise promotion. No mail/provider operation authorized.

Future tests include clean browser before choice (no optional calls/storage), refusal, granular choice, withdrawal, consent-version change, expired proof, unsubscribe and third-party leak inspection. NOT EXECUTED by policy writing. Current browser regression is bounded to synthetic accepted endpoints. Record actual storage/network evidence without raw tokens/person data and route unresolved legal provisions to [regulatory register](../compliance/regulatory-applicability.md).
