# Data-subject rights procedure

> HA-010-A documentation contract, 21 November 2026. [Authority](https://github.com/Shiva721254/HappyAlmere/issues/33#issuecomment-7065334379); [phase evidence and dispositions](../phases/HA-010-A.md). Documentation is not production certification, an operational exercise, or permission to process customer data.

PROD-A04; RUN-014. This is a future operational contract; no rights endpoint, export job or customer processing is introduced.

Intake through an approved contact with receipt/awareness timestamp, request scope and proportionate verification. Use existing authenticated proof when available; do not routinely demand an ID copy/BSN. If reasonable identity doubt persists, request only justified additional information through a protected channel. A private account workflow is not yet implemented.

Map the person/purpose across DATA01–20, databases, cache/index/files, provider systems, devices, logs and backups. Assess access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, objection, consent withdrawal and complaints separately. Document exceptions/legal holds and explain reasons; accounting retention does not justify retaining unrelated account details.

[GDPR Article12](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679) normally requires action information within one month; an additional two months may be justified with timely explanation in the first month. Internal30day deletion target is not the legal deadline. Privacy lead tracks each deadline and escalation; actual appointment remains pending.

Authorized execution must exclude other people's data from exports, use safe authenticated delivery, avoid secrets/hashes, propagate corrections/deletion to providers/publication/index/cache/devices, revoke affected sessions, record restricted outcome and notify requester. Withdrawal stops future consent-based processing; retain only minimal suppression/proof where justified.

Required future tests: verified positive request, wrong-person denial, third-party export exclusion, expired download rejection, legal-hold restriction/expiry, provider propagation, revoked-session denial and restored-deletion reconciliation before access. These are NOT TESTED here. Follow [retention](retention-schedule.md) and [incident](../security/incident-response.md) if a request reveals exposure. No real notification or filing authorized.
