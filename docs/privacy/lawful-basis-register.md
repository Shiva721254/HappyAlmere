# Purpose-specific lawful basis register

> HA-010-A documentation contract, 21 November 2026. [Authority](https://github.com/Shiva721254/HappyAlmere/issues/33#issuecomment-7065334379); [phase evidence and dispositions](../phases/HA-010-A.md). Documentation is not production certification, an operational exercise, or permission to process customer data.

PROD-A04, PROD-OPEN-08. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679) Articles5/6/9/10/12–14/25 govern the actual processing assessment. The following are candidate purposes, not approved legal conclusions or permission to collect. Owner privacy/legal lead must record necessity, alternatives, balancing where relevant, notice, evidence and review date before activation. Contract terms alone do not make processing necessary.

| Purpose / DATA IDs | Candidate basis and prerequisite | Minimized boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Requested account/service;01–04 | Art6(1)(b) only for objectively necessary service functions | No unrelated advertising profiling |
| Security/fraud;02–04,16–17,20 | Art6(1)(f) necessity/balancing or specific6(1)(c) duty | Minimal metadata, proportionate access/retention |
| Public directory;05,08 | Assess source, person's expectations and publication purpose; appropriate basis unresolved | Approved public fields; no mandatory home address |
| Organization/claim;06–07 | Contract or legitimate interests with authority/necessity evidence | Minimum claim proof; registration/email control not representative authority |
| Immediate discovery;09–10 | Requested search purpose; geo optional with manual alternative and separate permission assessment | No raw-search history/persistent precise location |
| Preferences/notifications;11–12 | Necessary requested feature or separate optional consent as applicable | Per-purpose settings and unsubscribe/revocation |
| Inquiry delivery;13 | Appropriate requester/recipient basis and transparent disclosure before delivery | No resale or unrelated recipients |
| Billing/tax;14–15 | Transaction necessity and exact statutory6(1)(c) obligation where applicable | Finance retention does not justify whole account retention |
| Tracking/marketing;10–12,19 | Prior valid consent where required; separate electronic-marketing rules | Disabled initially; no forced bundled consent |
| Backups;18 | Same justified recovery purpose, access safeguards and deletion reconciliation | Backup does not create a new unrestricted purpose |
| Aggregate improvement;19 | Actual anonymization assessment; otherwise personal-data basis still required | Suppress small/reidentifiable groups |

No sensitive targeting, routine BSN/ID copies or new special-category processing. Screen minors and national provisions before affected services; unresolved UAVG consolidated-text retrieval is explicitly retained in [regulatory register](../compliance/regulatory-applicability.md). [DPIA/DPO](dpia-dpo-screening.md) and provider/retention review must precede high-risk or new-purpose processing. Review every material change and before release; approval cannot waive law.
