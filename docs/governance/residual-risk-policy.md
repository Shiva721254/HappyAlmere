# Residual risk and acceptance authority

> HA-010-A documentation contract, 21 November 2026. [Authority](https://github.com/Shiva721254/HappyAlmere/issues/33#issuecomment-7065334379); [phase evidence and dispositions](../phases/HA-010-A.md). Documentation is not production certification, an operational exercise, or permission to process customer data.

PROD-A09; original risk definitions in [Issue33](https://github.com/Shiva721254/HappyAlmere/issues/33), existing canonical [risk register](../security/security-risk-register.md). HA010-specific evidence/dispositions live only in [phase record](../phases/HA-010-A.md); threats and this policy do not create parallel acceptance registers.

A risk record needs stable ID/source, affected release/asset/boundary, actual versus prospective evidence, likelihood/impact/severity rationale, accountable named owner, mitigation and verified test, residual exposure, alternative/disabled scope, expiry/review trigger and attributable independent recommendation/owner decision. Unknown exposure is not automatically low.

Applicable critical/high findings, auth bypass, active secret exposure, broken mandatory recovery, absent legal safeguards or missing authority block release. No agent can waive these, lower a threshold, suppress a test or declare a future capability verified. Eligible medium/low residuals may be accepted only by owner after independent review with bounded scope, tested mitigation and expiry. Approval never waives law, historical failures, DEF01–08 or future gates.

Reassess on changed code/dependency/provider/data/permission/domain, incident, expired mitigation or altered release scope. If accepted candidate content changes during integration/reconciliation, require new applicable exact-result verification and review; acceptance does not transfer automatically.

Documentation names roles but does not appoint operators or guarantee response coverage. Historical Identity performance acceptance remains bounded development with unresolved cause/tail risk; city STOP10/11 remains active. No production residual is accepted by HA010-A. Final handover triggers STOP12 before G10 independent review, G11 owner acceptance or separately authorized G12 integration.
